# RestoCafe 🍽️

## Executive Summary
**RestoCafe** is a responsive, user-centric restaurant website engineered to showcase culinary offerings, streamline table reservations, and communicate brand value. The platform highlights menu categories, pricing, chef profiles, service differentiators, and contact pathways, presenting a clear digital front door for diners, partners, and stakeholders. ## Strategic Objectives
This web application is designed to :
- Elevate online presence for restaurant services
- Increase customer engagement and table bookings
- Provide a structured experience across devices
- Present menu items with transparent pricing
- Act as a digital marketing asset for outreach and conversion
